**Production readiness & career launch.** The last mile: making a pipeline you'd trust on-call, and turning everything you've built into a job.

The graded levels here drill the patterns that separate a demo from a production system:

- **Idempotency** — reprocessing the same data must not double-count.
- **Retry with backoff** — transient failures are normal; back off, don't hammer.
- **Freshness SLAs** — know when data is stale *before* a stakeholder does.

And because a portfolio is only useful if someone sees it, this sprint ships **career guides** (read alongside the levels):

- 📄 `docs/career/interview-prep.md` — the data-engineering interview, topic by topic.
- 📄 `docs/career/system-design.md` — how to reason about a DE system-design question.
- 📄 `docs/career/portfolio-guide.md` — turn your passed sprints + proof artifacts into a portfolio that lands interviews.

Every sprint you've cleared is a real, graded project. That *is* your portfolio — this sprint helps you show it.
